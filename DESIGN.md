# FocusTube design

## Direction

Editorial study journal, not a generic productivity dashboard. Warm paper surfaces, dense ink typography, and signal green communicate calm focus and active filtering.

## Typography

- Interface: system sans-serif for reliable extension rendering
- Editorial display: Georgia for title moments and filtered-feed messaging
- Metadata: compact uppercase sans-serif with deliberate tracking

## Palette

- Paper: `#f3f0e7`
- Raised paper: `#fffdf7`
- Ink: `#172019`
- Muted ink: `#667066`
- Signal: `#b6f36b`
- Signal dark: `#27633b`
- Rule: `#ccd2c8`
- Warning: `#a44b35`

## Shape and spacing

- Crisp 1px rules and modest 8–12px corners
- Compact toolbar panel; no oversized cards or floating glass surfaces
- Clear vertical rhythm based on 4px increments
- Controls use full-width rows and strong text hierarchy

## Motion

- Short opacity reveal after classification
- No decorative animation
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Accessibility

- Minimum 4.5:1 text contrast
- Native buttons, labels, and inputs
- Visible keyboard focus ring
- Status updates use `aria-live`
